My Favorite Image

This is the photo where my friends and I hung out and had an amazing time together. It was a very memorable day because we celebrated my birthday at Manila Zoo. We spent several hours exploring the zoo and seeing many different kinds of animals. Some of our favorites were the tigers, monkeys, colorful birds, reptiles, and many other fascinating animals. It was exciting to learn more about them while taking pictures and enjoying each other's company. We laughed a lot, shared stories, and created wonderful memories throughout the day. After we finished exploring the zoo, we decided to walk all the way from Manila Zoo to Tondo. Even though it was a long walk, we enjoyed it because we kept talking, joking around, and enjoying the sights along the way. It was tiring, but spending time with my friends made the journey fun and worthwhile. When we finally arrived, we treated ourselves to a delicious samgyeopsal meal. We ate a lot, talked about our favorite moments from the day, and continued celebrating my birthday. It was one of the happiest birthdays I have ever had, and I will always remember the fun, laughter, and special memories we shared together.

My Favorite Video

Out of all the memories from my time at my old school, this one video easily takes the top spot. It was my friend Kurt’s birthday, and we knew we had to carry out the classic Filipino tradition of birthday egging. Me and the rest of the crew spent the whole day plotting our move, waiting patiently until the final bell rang and class was finally dismissed. A few of us quickly snuck over to the nearest sari-sari store to buy a fresh egg, while the others kept Kurt distracted so he wouldn't catch on to what was coming. Once the coast was clear, we cornered him, crack-egged him right on the head, and captured his entire priceless reaction on video. Beyond just the funny prank, that chaotic and hilarious moment captured how close we were as friends, easily locking itself in as one of my favorite core memories as a student.
